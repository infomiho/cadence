Scrubber prototype

Four variants of the player progress bar, on a simulated backend that behaves like librespot: position is reported every 250 ms and seeks only take effect after a round trip. Press play, then try each bar. The transport below is shared.

A. Today

current behaviour

5 px track, hit area equal to the painted strip, no thumb, no hover state. Seeks on press. The bar draws whatever the last report said.

B. Spotify-style

commit on release

4 px track inside a 12 px hit area, 12 px thumb revealed on hover or drag. Dragging previews the target; the seek fires once, on release.

D. Proposed for Cadence

recommended

Grow on hover like C, commit on release like B, plus a drag threshold, Escape to cancel, keyboard seeking, and a position model that owns its own clock so a stale sample can never drag the bar backwards. Click it and use ← → PgUp PgDn Home End.

  • No snap-back. Turn on the slow backend and compare against A: a requested position is held until a report agrees with it, so a stale sample cannot drag the bar backwards.
  • Drag threshold. A press that moves only a pixel or two is treated as a click, so a twitchy hand cannot turn a click into a scrub.
  • Escape cancels an in-flight drag and restores the position you started from.
  • Reduced motion drops the grow rather than animating it; the thumb still appears.
